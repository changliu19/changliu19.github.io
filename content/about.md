Chang Liu is an Assistant Professor at the School of Computer and Artificial Intelligence, <a class="org" href="https://english.sufe.edu.cn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Shanghai University of Finance and Economics</a> (SUFE). His research centers on **computer vision, machine learning, and multi-modal learning**, with a focus on referring and language-guided visual understanding, video object segmentation, and image / video matting and editing.

He received his B.Eng. from <a class="org" href="https://en.hit.edu.cn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Harbin Institute of Technology</a> (2018), and his M.Sc. (2019) and Ph.D. (2024) from <a class="org" href="https://www.ntu.edu.sg/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Nanyang Technological University</a>, Singapore. Before joining SUFE he was a Research Scientist at <a class="org" href="https://www.a-star.edu.sg/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">A\*STAR</a>, Singapore (2023–2024) and at <a class="org" href="https://www.bytedance.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">TikTok / ByteDance</a>, Singapore (2024–2025). He has published more than ten CCF-A papers at top venues including CVPR, ICCV, TPAMI, and TIP, gathering 2,000+ citations.
